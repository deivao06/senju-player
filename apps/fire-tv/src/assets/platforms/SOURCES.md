# Platform identifiers

Five small local PNG wordmarks from Wikimedia Commons SVG previews, downloaded
30 October 2026. Original artwork belongs to the respective platforms. Used only
to identify categories supplied by the user's playlist; Senju does not provide
content or claim affiliation. Attribution/source and SHA-256 are in each
`*.png.json` sidecar. No generated or reconstructed logos.

- Netflix: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Netflix_2015_logo.svg
- Globoplay: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Globoplay_logo.svg
- Prime Video: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Prime_Video_logo_(2024).svg
- Disney+: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Disney%2B_logo.svg
- HBO: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HBO_logo.svg

Files are documented by Commons as text/simple logo graphics; trademarks remain
with their owners. Prime, Disney+ and HBO use a native monochrome light tint on
dark surfaces for contrast. PNGs total ~59 KiB and are bundled, not fetched at runtime.
